Welcome to ConfQuest

You have 3 minutes on the conference floor. Walk into a room and press E (or tap Enter Room) to answer its trivia question. A right answer earns that room badge.

Move with the arrow keys or WASD. On a touch screen, use the buttons under the floor map.

Arrows / WASD to move. E to enter a room. Esc to skip a question.
